XSniper iPhone app (free, no paid Apple account)

1. Make a free GitHub account, create a NEW PUBLIC repository (public = free Mac build minutes),
   and upload everything in this folder (keep the .github folder).
2. Repo > Actions > "Build IPA" > Run workflow. Wait ~5 minutes.
3. Open the finished run and download the "XSniper-ipa" artifact. Unzip it to get XSniper.ipa.
4. On your Windows PC install Sideloadly (sideloadly.io) and iTunes + iCloud from apple.com (not the Store versions).
5. Plug the iPhone in, drag XSniper.ipa into Sideloadly, sign in with your normal free Apple ID, press Start.
6. On the iPhone: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > trust your Apple ID. Turn on Developer Mode if asked.
7. Open XSniper, paste the link from XSniper on your PC (Settings > Phone web server > Copy link).

Free Apple IDs expire the app every 7 days: just re-run Sideloadly (or use SideStore/AltStore to refresh automatically).

BACKGROUND BUZZ (new)
- Paste the FULL link (with ?t=...) so the app can listen on its own. Allow notifications when asked.
- With the app running, a silent audio loop keeps it alive in the background and every snipe becomes a notification with sound and a buzz. Tap it to open Roblox.
- Leave the app open once (don't swipe it away from the app switcher) and keep the phone on the same WiFi as the PC.
- iOS can still stop it (low battery, memory pressure, another app taking over audio). If the buzzes stop, open XSniper again.
- Silent mode: the sound is muted by the ring switch, but the notification still vibrates.
